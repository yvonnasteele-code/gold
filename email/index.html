NEWSLETTER
November 2026
MOVING THE NEEDLE
India imports 86% of its gold. NITI Aayog sat down with PIF on how to change that.
On 10th November, NITI Aayog’s Vice Chairman, Dr Ashok Kumar Lahiri, and CEO, Sh Anurag Jain, deliberated on our forthcoming report, India’s Gold Mining Policy: Challenges, Learnings and the Road Ahead, with PIF and the World Gold Council.
INSIDE THIS ISSUE
1 The meeting. Who was at the table, and why it matters for India’s gold mining future.
2 Shared priorities and a next step. Better geological data, deeper exploration, serious bidders and a financial regime that keeps projects bankable.
3 In the news. The Economic Times, The Times of India and others on a plan in the works to ramp up gold mining in India.
